Moderator Certification Examination

Testing & Qualification Platform

Welcome

This examination evaluates a candidate's ability to moderate an online messaging platform according to the following core policies:

  • All messages and actions must be age appropriate.
  • All messages and actions must not be racist, homophobic, transphobic, ableist, or discriminatory.
  • All messages and actions must not request personal information and must comply with applicable U.S. laws.

The examination consists of:

20 Policy Questions

Multiple-choice moderation scenarios.

Chat Log Review

Analyze conversations and determine appropriate actions.

Written Responses

Explain moderation decisions in detail.

Candidate Information

Administrator Access
